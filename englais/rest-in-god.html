Tonight

Theme: Rest in God

Jesus Blesses the Children

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Mark 10:13-16
Psalm 62:5-8

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord Jesus, as this day ends I come to you with a grateful heart. Thank you for every moment of the day, for the work completed, for the bread on the table, and for the people you placed in my path. Even in the difficult moments, your hand was with me and you did not let me walk alone.

I now hand over to you what worried me today and what I left unfinished. You know my weariness, my doubts, and the thoughts that will not let me be at peace. I want to lay them at your feet, like a child resting in his father’s arms. Teach me to trust without so many questions and to receive your kingdom with simplicity.

Watch over my family tonight and everyone I love. Bless the children, the sick, the elderly, and those who feel alone. Give rest to those who suffer and peace to those who cannot sleep. May I wake tomorrow with new strength, knowing that my life is safe in your hands. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

One day, on the road toward Judea, some people were bringing children to Jesus so that he might touch them and bless them. They were mothers and fathers who knew that this Teacher was different from the others. But the disciples, thinking Jesus was too busy to attend to the little ones, rebuked them and tried to send them away.

When Jesus saw it, he was indignant with his disciples and said to them: “Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them; for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.” Those words must have surprised everyone. In that time, children had no place of importance in adult conversations, but to Jesus they were the closest to his kingdom.

Then he added something that changed the way faith is understood: “Truly I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.” A child does not come to his father with a résumé or a list of merits. He comes with open hands, with trust, knowing he will be welcomed. This is how Jesus wants us to come to him.

And the account ends with a very tender image: Jesus took the children in his arms, put his hands on them, and blessed them. It was not a hurried gesture. He stopped, embraced them, and prayed for each one. Today, at the end of the day, that same blessing is open to you. You do not have to prove anything; only come near, let go of your burdens, and allow yourself to be embraced by the love of God.

✝️ Today’s Message

Psalm 62 tells us: “My soul finds rest in God alone.” That word, rest, is very precious at the end of a day. We are not speaking only of the body sleeping, but of the soul finding a safe place to stop. And that place, according to the psalmist, is God himself, our rock, our salvation, and our refuge.

Many times we arrive at night with a mind full of unfinished business. We replay conversations, calculate what remains, and worry about what is coming. Jesus, in welcoming the children, shows us another way: the way of simple trust. A child in his father’s arms does not solve the world’s problems, but he rests because he knows someone stronger is holding him.

Tonight you can practice that rest in a very practical way. Take a few minutes before bed, turn off the screens, and make a short list of the things that trouble you. Then pray over each one and hand it to the Lord in simple words. If something can wait until tomorrow, leave it there. If there is someone you need to be reconciled with, decide to speak with them at daybreak.

The psalmist invites us to pour out our hearts before God, because he is our refuge. You do not have to pretend to be strong before him. You can tell him that you are tired, that you are afraid, or that you do not understand something. And then you can rest, not because everything is resolved, but because God is with you. His love holds you tonight, as it held the children Jesus took in his arms.

As you close this day, give thanks for what you lived and leave in God’s hands everything you could not resolve. He keeps watch while you sleep and never tires of caring for you. Rest in peace, and may his blessing cover you tonight like a quiet embrace.
