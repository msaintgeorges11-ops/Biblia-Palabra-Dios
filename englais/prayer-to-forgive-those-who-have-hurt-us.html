🙏 Prayer for God to Help Us Forgive Those Who Have Wronged Us

A forgiveness that sets us free · Bible Word God

Introduction

Forgiving can be one of the hardest tasks in life. When someone betrays us, humiliates us or hurts us deeply, the pain does not disappear just because we wish it would. Very often, resentment becomes a burden we drag everywhere. Yet the Bible teaches us that forgiveness is a path to freedom, not to weakness. Today we will see how to ask God for the strength to forgive, one step at a time.

Matthew 6:14-15: Forgiveness Received and Forgiveness Given

Matthew 6:14-15“For if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you, but if you do not forgive others their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.”
📖 Read the Whole of Chapter 6

Jesus spoke these words right after teaching the Lord’s Prayer. He reminds us that whoever has received so great a forgiveness cannot cling to resentment without their heart growing hard. The message is not that we earn God’s forgiveness by our efforts, but that a heart that refuses to forgive shows that it has not yet understood the immensity of the grace it has received. To forgive is to recognize that God has been merciful to us and to desire to imitate him.

Ephesians 4:32: Forgiving as God Forgave Us

Ephesians 4:32“Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.”

Here Paul joins three attitudes: kindness, compassion and forgiveness. To forgive does not mean pretending that what happened did not matter, or exposing ourselves to harm again. It means giving up the desire for revenge and handing justice over to God. One can forgive and, at the same time, set healthy boundaries. Forgiveness is a decision that, with time, also becomes a journey of the heart. Whoever forgives stops being a prisoner of their past.

How to Move Toward Forgiveness

  • Acknowledge your pain honestly; do not deny it or minimize it.
  • Ask God for the strength to forgive, even if you do not yet feel it.
  • Give up revenge and hand justice over to God.
  • Pray for the person who hurt you; it is one of the most powerful ways to heal from resentment.
  • Set healthy boundaries and, if there is danger or abuse, seek appropriate protection and support.

A Message of Faith, Hope and Trust

Forgiveness does not change the past, but it transforms the future. When you decide to forgive, you stop carrying the weight of bitterness and you make room for peace. You do not have to accomplish it in a single day; you can say to God: “Help me to forgive.” He knows your pain and walks with you on this path. His hands heal wounds that seemed incurable. If today all you can say is “I wish I wanted to forgive,” that is already a precious beginning. God takes that sincere desire and gradually transforms it, in his time, with a tenderness that neither humiliates nor rushes.

Prayer

Lord, you know the wound I carry in my heart. You know who hurt me and how much I have suffered. Sometimes I feel anger, sadness and the wish that this person would pay for what they did. Today I do not come with perfect words, but with sincerity: I do not know how to forgive, but I want to.

Father, you have forgiven me much. Give me a merciful heart, able to let go of resentment. Heal my memories, calm my emotions and take away from me the desire for revenge. I place in your hands the one who offended me; do justice with your wisdom and soften my heart toward them. Teach me to set healthy boundaries without hating. May bitterness not rule my days, and may your peace take its place. Thank you for the freedom you give me when I forgive. Help me to live in it every day. In Jesus’ name, amen.

Conclusion

Forgiving is an act of courage that sets us free. Today you can take the first step, even if it is a very small one. God accompanies the one who decides to let go of resentment and fills them with a peace that no one can take away.
