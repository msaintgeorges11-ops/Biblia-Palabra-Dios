William Carey Arrives in India

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Mark 16:15
Psalm 96:1-4

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord God, as this day begins I lift my voice to give you thanks. You are great and worthy of all praise, and your love reaches the farthest places on earth. Thank you for life, for the health you give me, and for the opportunity to serve you today where you have placed me.

I ask you to kindle in me a willing heart. May I not stay shut up in my own affairs, but look with compassion on those around me and remember that your Word is for all peoples. Give me courage to speak of you simply and gently, and give me a heart that loves people as you love them.

Bless the missionaries, the translators of your Word, and all who serve in difficult places. Give them strength, protection, and joy. And bless also my home, my work, and the people I will speak with today. Guide me with your Spirit so that each of my gestures reflects your goodness. May this day be an occasion to proclaim your wonders. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

On November 11, 1793, the English missionary William Carey landed in Calcutta, India. He had been born in 1761 in a village in England, into a humble family, and worked as a shoemaker before becoming a Baptist pastor. From his youth he was interested in learning about the peoples of the world and in learning languages.

Carey was convinced that Christians should take the gospel to all nations, an idea that was not accepted by everyone in his day. In 1792 he published a work on the obligation of Christians to use means for spreading the gospel among the peoples who did not know it. It is remembered that in a sermon that year he urged with these words: “Expect great things from God; attempt great things for God.”

In April 1793 he left England with his family and his companion John Thomas, sent by the Baptist missionary society that had been formed for that purpose. After several months of travel, they arrived in Calcutta in November. The beginnings were not easy: the East India Company viewed missionaries with suspicion, and Carey had to face financial hardship, illness, and an unfamiliar language.

With patience he devoted himself to learning Bengali and other languages, and in time he settled in Serampore, a Danish colony, together with other colleagues. There, translations of the Bible were printed that came to be published, in whole or in part, in dozens of languages and dialects. Carey’s work, which began with an uncertain journey, helped awaken the modern missionary movement and reminds us that Jesus’ command is still in force.

✝️ Today’s Message

Jesus said to his disciples: “Go into all the world and preach the gospel to all creation.” This word was not just for a few heroes of history, but for all of us who follow the Lord. The story of William Carey reminds us that many great works begin with a simple decision: to obey what God has said, even if the road is uncertain.

At first glance, the command to go into all the world might seem distant to us. Perhaps we think our world is our home, our workplace, our neighborhood, or our school. And that is exactly where it begins. The psalmist says it this way: “Sing to the Lord, bless his name; tell of his salvation from day to day.” Notice those words: from day to day. Proclaiming God does not always mean traveling far; it means living each day so that others see his goodness.

Today you can put this into practice in concrete ways. Greet warmly someone who usually goes unnoticed. Offer your help without expecting anything in return. If someone asks how you are, answer with gratitude and not with complaint. And if a conversation opens, simply share what God has done in your life. You do not need long speeches; a sincere testimony is enough.

Carey also teaches us perseverance. His beginnings were slow and full of trials, but he kept working, translating, and teaching. Do not be discouraged if what you do today seems small. God uses small things for great ones. Attempt something for him in faith, trust in his help, and wait with hope. Declare his glory among the nations, as the psalm says, and leave the result in his hands.

Walk today with the certainty that the same God who accompanied his servants in distant lands goes with you to every place. May his grace give you courage for every task and his promises sustain you when your strength runs short. His faithfulness covers you from now on.
