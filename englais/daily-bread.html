Manna in the Wilderness

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Exodus 16:11-15
Psalm 4:6-8

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord, as this day ends I come before you with a grateful heart. Thank you for today’s bread, for the roof over my head, for the people who were with me, and for every moment I felt your hand upon me. Even in the hard hours, you were with me and did not let me fall. Everything good I received comes from you.

Forgive me for the times I worried too much, for the words I should not have said, and for the moments I doubted your care. Like your people in the wilderness, sometimes I want to secure tomorrow before giving thanks for today. Teach me to receive each day as a gift and to rest in the knowledge that you will provide what I need, in your time and in your way.

Now I hand over to you my burdens: what was left unfinished, what worries me, and the people I love. Keep my family through this night. Give me peaceful sleep and a heart at peace, because you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety. May I wake tomorrow with new strength and renewed faith, ready to walk with you again. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

After leaving Egypt, the people of Israel were walking through the wilderness of Sin. There were thousands of them, with children and the elderly, and soon they began to feel hunger. Afraid and weary, they grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying they would rather have stayed in Egypt, where they had bread. They had seen the plagues and the parted sea, but hunger made them forget so many miracles.

God heard the complaints and responded with kindness. He told Moses that he would rain bread from heaven, and that each day the people would go out and gather that day’s portion. That same evening quail came up and covered the camp. The next morning, when the dew had evaporated, there appeared on the surface of the wilderness a fine, flake-like thing, as fine as frost on the ground.

When they saw it, the Israelites asked one another: “What is this?” because they did not know what it was. Moses answered them: “It is the bread that the Lord has given you to eat.” He gave a simple instruction: each one was to gather what he needed for that day. God was teaching them to depend on him each day, and not on their own reserves.

The people ate that bread for forty years, until they reached the promised land. Every morning was a new lesson: the God who brought them out of Egypt had not forgotten them. His faithfulness did not depend on the greatness of the people’s faith, but on the greatness of his love.

✝️ Today’s Message

Manna teaches us that God cares for his people one day at a time. He did not give Israel a storehouse full for many years, but a fresh provision every morning. This has a purpose: that his children learn to look to him, and not to their reserves, as the source of life. Jesus taught the same thing when he invited us to ask the Father for our daily bread.

Perhaps tonight you lie down with worries about tomorrow: a pending bill, an illness, a child who is far away, an uncertain job. Anxiety asks us to carry today the weight of all our tomorrows, but God did not give you strength to carry it all at once. He gave you this day and accompanied you through it. Tomorrow will also have its bread for you.

The psalmist wrote: “In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety.” Here is a practical application for tonight, before you sleep. First, name aloud three provisions from God you received today, however small. Second, write on a piece of paper what worries you about tomorrow and hand it over to God in prayer, like leaving a package in the hands of a good friend. Third, turn off the light giving thanks, without going over your problems again.

Sleeping in peace does not mean there are no problems, but that we know who stays awake watching over us. The one who fed an entire people in the wilderness will know how to feed you. Rest easy: God’s faithfulness is new every morning.

Thank you, Lord, for this day that is ending. Now leave your burdens at the feet of Christ, because he cares for you, and rest with a calm heart. May his peace cover you through the night, and tomorrow may you wake with new strength and hope.
