Theme: A Light in the Night

Nicodemus Visits Jesus by Night

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

John 3:1-2
John 3:16-17

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord Jesus, as night falls, I come to you with a grateful heart. Thank you for this day, for the bread on the table, for the people who surrounded me, and for every moment when I felt your hand on my life. Even in the hard hours you were near, and today I want to acknowledge it with humility.

Like Nicodemus, I come to you by night, with questions and weariness. There are things I have not understood, decisions that still weigh on me, and matters I could not settle. I bring no answers; I bring only what I have. I give you my doubts, my hurry, and my worries for the people I love. You know what I cannot put into words, and so I rest, knowing you understand me better than I understand myself.

Thank you, because you loved the world so much that you gave your Son, and because you did not send him to condemn but to save. This truth gives me peace. Forgive what I did wrong today and what I failed to do. Keep my home, my family, and those who sleep far from me. Give me peaceful sleep and a waking with renewed hope. I place in your hands everything that remains unresolved, trusting in your kindness. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

The Gospel of John tells us there was a man named Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a ruler of the Jews. He was a respected teacher, knowledgeable in the Scriptures, with a position to protect. Yet something in the words and works of Jesus troubled him. He had questions he could not resolve from his teacher’s chair, and he decided to seek out Jesus in person.

John points out an important detail: Nicodemus came to Jesus by night. Perhaps he wanted to talk without the pressure of the crowd, or perhaps he feared what his colleagues would say. Whatever the reason, he was sincere. He told Jesus that they knew he had come from God as a teacher, for no one could do the signs he did unless God was with him.

Jesus did not send him away or criticize him for coming in secret. He welcomed him and spoke to him about being born again, a deep change that only God can work in the heart. Nicodemus did not understand everything at that moment, and he said so frankly. The conversation contained the best-known words in the Bible: that God so loved the world that he gave his only Son, so that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.

Nicodemus’s story does not end that night. Later we see him defending Jesus with caution before the Pharisees, and finally helping with his burial. That nighttime visit was the beginning of a light that grew little by little. That is why it encourages us: no one comes to Jesus seeing everything clearly, and the night is no obstacle to meeting him.

✝️ Today’s Message

There are hours of the day when questions weigh more heavily, and night is often one of them. When everything falls silent, the unfinished matters, the conversations we replay in our minds, and the fears without a name come to the surface. Nicodemus knew that feeling well. That is why he went to find Jesus by night, and found in him an open door.

The first thing this story teaches is that you do not have to put your thoughts in order before approaching God. Nicodemus arrived with questions and left with a seed of faith. You too can come with your doubts just as they are. God is not scandalized by a clumsy prayer, a weary silence, or a heart that does not yet understand.

The second is that God’s love is the greatest answer to anxiety. John reminds us that God sent his Son not to condemn the world, but so that the world might be saved through him. If God did this for you, you can trust that he will also care for your family, your work, and your future. What you did not settle today is not a sign of abandonment.

As a practice for tonight, do three simple things. Write on a piece of paper the worries running through your head and hand them over to God out loud. Give thanks for three concrete things that happened today, however small. Then read John 3:16 slowly and stay for a moment in silence. Lie down knowing that the light of Christ does not go out when night falls.

As you close this day, hand over to the Father what you could not settle, and accept his quiet embrace. He keeps watch while you sleep, and his love is greater than any worry. Rest, for tomorrow his mercy will rise with you.
