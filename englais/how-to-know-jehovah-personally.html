How to Know Jehovah Personally

From knowing about God to truly knowing him · Bible Word God

Knowing Is More Than Knowing About

It is possible to know many facts about God without actually knowing him personally. It is like knowing a great deal about a famous person without ever having met them. The Bible invites us to something far deeper: a living, daily, life-changing relationship with Jehovah, our Creator.

Jeremiah expressed what God values: “Let him who boasts boast of this, that he understands and knows me, that I am Jehovah” (Jeremiah 9:24). Knowing him is the most important thing we can attain.

The Way Opened by Jesus Christ

Jesus said: “And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent” (John 17:3). Access to God comes through his Son. He declared that he is the way, the truth and the life, and that no one comes to the Father except through him (John 14:6).

Knowing God begins with recognizing our need, believing in Jesus and receiving the forgiveness he offers. Through faith we pass from being strangers to being beloved children.

Speaking to God in Prayer

No relationship grows without conversation. Prayer is speaking to God, and it requires no complicated formula. You can give thanks, confess, ask, praise or simply sit in his presence. Jesus taught us to pray simply and to call God “Father.”

A good habit is to set aside a fixed time each day, even ten minutes. With perseverance, prayer stops being a chore and becomes the meeting place with the one who loves us most.

Listening to God in His Word

God speaks through the Bible. As we read it, we discover his character, his promises and his will. The psalmist said: “Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path” (Psalm 119:105). The point is not to read at top speed, but to meditate, to ask questions and to obey.

You can begin with the Gospel of John, continue with the Psalms, then explore other books. In our reading section you can read for free, chapter by chapter. Ask the Holy Spirit to enlighten you.

Seeking with All Your Heart

God makes a wonderful promise: “You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart” (Jeremiah 29:13). And James confirms it: “Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you” (James 4:8). God does not hide from the one who seeks him sincerely.

Seeking with all your heart involves consistency, humility and a willingness to change. It means letting God teach us, even when he corrects us.

Obeying and Walking with Others

Jesus said that whoever loves his word will be loved by the Father, and that he and the Father would come and make their home with that person (John 14:23). Obedience is not a burden, but the path of communion. Every step of faithfulness deepens our knowledge of God.

We also need a community. The Bible encourages us not to give up meeting together (Hebrews 10:25). A healthy church helps us grow, serve and persevere.

A First Step Today

If you wish to begin this relationship, you can pray like this: “Jehovah, my God, I want to know you. I acknowledge that I need you. Thank you for sending Jesus Christ. Forgive my sins and guide me. Teach me to listen to your voice. Amen.”

If you prayed with all your heart, talk about it with someone you trust and begin reading the Bible every day. God will welcome you. And if you would like us to pray with you, you can write to us from the contact page. This is the beginning of a relationship that will last forever.

Signs of Growth

How can we know whether we are growing in the knowledge of God? Here are some signs: a greater desire to pray and to read his Word, more peace in trials, more love for others, more sensitivity to sin and more gratitude. It is not about perfection but about direction. Every day devoted to seeking God, however modest it may seem, is a step toward a deeper relationship. Keep going, because he never tires of welcoming you.
