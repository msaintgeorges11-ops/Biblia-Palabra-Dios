Tonight

Theme: Consider the Lilies

The Ravens and the Lilies of the Field

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Luke 12:24-28
Proverbs 16:3

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord Jesus, I come to the end of the day with many things on my mind and tired feet. Thank you for accompanying me in my work, in conversations and in the rush. Thank you for the health that allowed me to carry out my tasks and for the moments of joy you gave me, even the small ones I barely noticed. I acknowledge that every good thing comes from your hand and that I lacked nothing.

Forgive me for the times today I fretted and worried about tomorrow, as if everything depended on me alone. Often I run so much that I stop looking at the birds and flowers that speak to me of your care. I give you my list of pending things, what I did and what I could not do. I commit my works to you, for you know what is important and what can wait until tomorrow. Take from me the anxiety of accomplishing everything.

Give me now true rest, not only of the body, but also of the soul. Take care of my family through this night, bless those who work in the early hours and console those who cannot find peace. Tomorrow, may my day begin by trusting the Father who feeds the birds and clothes the flowers. I sleep in your peace. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

One day, surrounded by a great crowd, Jesus spoke to his disciples about something that concerns every human being: what to eat, what to wear, how to get by tomorrow. He told them not to be anxious about their life, nor about what they would eat, nor about what they would put on, for life is more than food and the body more than clothing.

Then he invited them to look around: “Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap, they have no storeroom or barn, and yet God feeds them. Of how much more value are you than the birds!” The disciples were simple people, many of them fishermen and workers, who knew what it was to live day to day. Jesus was not asking them to stop working, but not to live with a tight heart.

Then he asked them a question that still touches us: “Which of you by being anxious can add a single cubit to his span of life?” And he led them to look at the flowers of the field: “Consider how the lilies grow: they neither toil nor spin; yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.” If God so clothes the grass that is alive in the field today and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, how much more will he care for his children?

Further on, in the same passage, Jesus added words of tenderness: “Fear not, little flock, for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom.” The teaching is simple and profound: the Father who cares for the birds and the lilies cares far more for you. Looking at creation can teach us to trust, and trusting allows us to rest at the end of the day.

✝️ Today’s Message

If you are like most of us, your day was filled with things to do, and tomorrow’s list is probably already there waiting for you. It is normal that at nightfall the mind keeps circling around bills, pending decisions and what might go wrong. Jesus knew this feeling well, and that is why he spoke about worry so clearly to those who followed him.

Notice that Jesus did not say “do not worry about it.” He said “consider,” that is, look carefully. Look at the ravens that God feeds, look at the lilies he clothes. Worry shuts us up in our thoughts, but contemplation opens our eyes to God’s care that already exists around us. And if he cares for them, you are worth much more to him.

Proverbs 16:3 gives us a practical guide: “Commit your work to the Lord, and your plans will be established.” To commit is to hand over with trust. Tonight, you can take your pending things and place them one by one before God, asking him to show you which are really important. What could not be done today is not a condemnation; it is an opportunity to begin tomorrow with a clearer mind.

Before sleeping, spend ten minutes without screens or noise. Go to a window if you can, or recall something beautiful you saw today: a tree, a bird, the face of a loved one. Give thanks for it, breathe slowly and stay a moment in silence. Then turn off the light with the certainty that your worth does not depend on what you accomplished today, but on the love of the Father who cares for you.

Thank you, Lord, for every step of this day. Let go of your worries now and let your heart fill with calm, for the Father who cares for the birds of the air cares for you too while you sleep. May your night be serene and your waking full of hope.
