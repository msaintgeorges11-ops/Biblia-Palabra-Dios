Jehovah Is Love, Justice and Power

Three traits of God’s character · Bible Word God

A Perfect Character

When we think about what God is like, we tend to emphasize one trait and forget the others. Some see only a severe God who judges; others, a gentle God who lets everything slide. The Bible presents a fuller picture: Jehovah is love, justice and power, and these three attributes are harmonized in perfect unity.

By knowing these three qualities, our faith becomes more balanced and our prayer life deeper.

When God Describes Himself

On Mount Sinai, God passed before Moses and proclaimed his name: “The LORD, The LORD God, merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abundant in goodness and truth” (Exodus 34:6). This is the best definition of his character, given by God himself. It blends tenderness and firmness in a single sentence.

This text is later quoted by prophets and psalmists as an anchor of hope. Even in punishment, God remains faithful to what he is.

Jehovah Is Love

John says it plainly: “God is love” (1 John 4:8). He does not possess love like someone who has it from time to time, but his very nature is to love. In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word “hesed” expresses a faithful, covenant love that does not give up. It is the love that pursues the one who has wandered away.

Its highest expression is the cross: “But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us” (Romans 5:8).

Jehovah Is Justice

God’s love does not ignore evil. His justice means that he always does what is right and does not leave wickedness unanswered. The psalmist says: “Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne” (Psalm 89:14). This should comfort us: the God who governs the world is not unjust.

For those who have suffered abuse or deception, knowing that God is just offers hope. For those who have done wrong, divine justice calls us to repentance and reminds us how much we need grace.

Jehovah Is Power

God is almighty. Jeremiah prayed: “Ah Lord GOD! behold, thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power and stretched out arm, and there is nothing too hard for thee” (Jeremiah 32:17). His power was shown in creation, in the plagues of Egypt, in the crossing of the sea and, above all, in the resurrection of Jesus.

This power is neither arbitrary nor cruel. It is at the service of his love and his justice. The same God who governs the galaxies bends down to support the weary.

Three Attributes, One Person

Jeremiah 9:24 brings these ideas together: “I am the LORD which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these things I delight” (Jeremiah 9:24). Steadfast love, justice and righteousness. God delights in them and practices them all at once.

In Christ we see these three attributes united: the love that led him to the cross, the justice that was satisfied there and the power that raised him from the dead.

How to Live This Truth

When your heart doubts God, remember his three attributes. If you feel little loved, think of his love. If you feel that evil is triumphing, remember his justice. If it seems to you that nothing can change, remember his power.

We invite you to pray like this: “Jehovah, thank you because you are love, justice and power. Help me to trust you in every situation and to reflect your character before others.” God answers with joy the prayer of one who desires to be like him.

A Balance We Need

Today we need to remember these three attributes together. Love without justice would be permissiveness; justice without love would be cruelty; power without the other two would be tyranny. In Jehovah, the three complete one another perfectly. That is why we can trust him without fearing that he will abandon us or treat us badly. As you read the Bible, observe how each story reveals one or more of these traits, and give thanks for the God who is perfect in everything.
