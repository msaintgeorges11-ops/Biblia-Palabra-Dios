The Angel Announces to Zechariah

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Luke 1:11-14
Proverbs 13:12

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord God, at the end of this day, I come before you with a grateful heart. Thank you for today’s sustenance, for the people you placed on my path, for the strength you gave me and for every little blessing I may not have noticed while running here and there. Everything good comes from your hand, and today I want to acknowledge it.

I now give you the prayers still awaiting an answer. You know how long I have been asking, and you also know the weariness of waiting. Teach me to believe that your times are perfect and that you do not forget me. Take from my heart bitterness and haste, and fill it with hope. Forgive me for the times I doubted your goodness, and for the moments I wanted to settle things by myself.

Grant me now deep rest. I lay at your feet the burdens of the day, the worries of tomorrow and everything I could not settle. Take care of my family while we sleep, protect our homes and console those who weep tonight. When I wake, may my soul be renewed and ready to serve you. Thank you, because you always listen to me. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

Zechariah was an elderly priest who lived in the time of King Herod. He and his wife Elizabeth were righteous before God and walked blamelessly in his commandments. But they had a very great sorrow: Elizabeth had not been able to have children, and now both were advanced in years. For years, they had prayed, and the answer seemed not to come.

One day, it fell to Zechariah by lot to enter the sanctuary of the Lord to burn incense, while the people prayed outside. Suddenly, at the right side of the altar, an angel of the Lord appeared to him. Zechariah was troubled and fear fell upon him. But the angel said to him: “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer has been heard.”

That prayer that seemed forgotten had been heard from the beginning. The angel announced that Elizabeth would bear a son, who would be named John, and that he would be a joy and delight to many. This child would later be the one who prepared the way for the Lord Jesus.

Zechariah had difficulty believing, and so he remained mute until the child’s birth. Yet God’s promise was fulfilled just as it had been spoken. This story teaches us that waiting does not mean abandonment. God listens, keeps every prayer and answers at the moment he knows to be best, very often when we no longer expect it.

✝️ Today’s Message

When the day ends, many hearts are left thinking about what has not yet arrived: a healing, a job, the reconciliation of a family, an answer. The proverb describes it truthfully: hope deferred makes the heart sick. God does not mock this pain; he understands it.

The story of Zechariah reminds us that heaven did not ignore his prayer. For years it seemed that nothing was happening, but God was preparing something greater than what he had asked for. He would not receive only a son; he would be the father of the one who would announce the coming of the Savior. Sometimes the delay is the time in which God prepares a bigger plan.

The same proverb adds that a desire fulfilled is a tree of life. When God answers, he brings freshness, joy and new strength. But while the answer is on its way, he does not leave us alone. He sustains us by his presence, by his Word and by his peace. To wait in faith is not to stand with arms crossed, but to keep loving, serving and praying with trust.

Tonight, do something practical. Write on a piece of paper a prayer still awaiting an answer and give it to God in simple words. Then give thanks for one thing you have already received today. This trains the heart to wait with gratitude rather than with anguish. Tell the Lord: “I trust in your timing.” Then rest, for the one who keeps your prayers neither slumbers nor sleeps, and his faithfulness does not fail.

End this day with a grateful heart, for God has heard your words and keeps each one of your hopes. Leave in his hands what you could not change and receive his rest. May the Lord give you quiet sleep and a dawn full of peace.
