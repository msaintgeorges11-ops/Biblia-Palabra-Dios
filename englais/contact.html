We're here to listen

Thank you for visiting Bible Word God. This page exists so you can reach us easily, whether to ask a question, share a suggestion, let us know about a mistake, or simply tell us how the Word of God has helped you. We read every message carefully and try to reply as soon as we can.

Contact details

You can write to us at any time; we take the time to read and answer each message with care. If you prefer to call, feel free to do so, and if we can't pick up at that moment, leave us a message or send us an email so we can get back to you.

What can you write to us about?

Questions about the site. If you can't find a book, a chapter or a feature, or you don't know how to use the search or the audio, tell us and we'll guide you step by step.

Mistakes and corrections. If you find a misspelled word, a verse out of place, a broken link or a problem opening a page on your phone, let us know. Tell us which page, and if you can, your device model; that helps us fix it faster.

Suggestions. We want to know what you would like to see: a book explained, a topic, a new feature, another voice for the audio. Many improvements to the site come from readers' ideas.

Articles. If you would like us to write about a book, a person or a passage of the Bible, tell us. We are preparing one article for each book, and your requests help us decide the order.

Prayer requests. If you are going through a difficult time and would like someone to pray with you, you can write to us. We are not a professional counseling service, but we will gladly lift your need to the Lord.

Offerings and support. If you had any problem with the offering button or would like to know how to support this work, you can ask us as well.

Collaboration. If you have a Christian project that could complement ours, or you would like to propose a respectful collaboration, we are open to talking.

How to write a good message

To help you better, we recommend including your name, the subject of your message and any necessary details. If it is about a mistake, tell us which page it happened on and what you expected to see. If it is a Bible question, mention the book, chapter and verse. The clearer you are, the more useful our reply will be.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a reply? We try to reply as soon as possible. If your message is simple, we can usually answer in a short time; if it needs review, we will let you know.

Do you charge for reading the Bible? No. Reading, search and audio are free. The offering is voluntary.

Do I need to create an account? No. You can use the site freely without registering.

What do you do with my data? We only use your email or phone number to reply to you. You can read the details in our privacy policy.

Can you guide me spiritually? We will gladly share what the Bible says about your question. For serious situations, we encourage you to also speak with a pastor or a trusted leader from your local church and, in a medical or safety emergency, with the emergency services in your country.

Before writing to us

Many questions are answered in a few seconds within the site itself. If you are looking for a verse, type the reference into the search box on the home page, for example Psalms 23. If you want to read a whole book, open the Old or New Testament tab, open the book and choose the chapter. If you want to listen, tap the audio button inside the chapter and choose the voice you prefer.

If you want to understand a book of the Bible better, visit the articlessection, where we explain who wrote it, for whom, and what it teaches. And if you want to know more about who we are and why we do this, you can read the About.

Our commitment to you

Every message we receive deserves respect, whether it is a simple question, a criticism or a word of thanks. We commit to reading it carefully, replying kindly and treating your information with discretion. We will not share your email or phone number with anyone, and we will only use them to reply to you.

We also want to be honest about our limits. We are a small team, and sometimes a message may take longer than we would like. If you don't get a reply within a few days, feel free to write to us again; your message may have been lost among others. Your patience and understanding are a great blessing to us.

One last message

Behind this site are people who believe that the Word of God is still alive and still speaks today. If something you read here blesses you, we are very glad to hear it. And if something can be improved, we want to know that too. Thank you for being part of this; write to us whenever you like.
