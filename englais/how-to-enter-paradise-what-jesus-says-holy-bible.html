📚 How to Enter Paradise - What Jesus Says in the Holy Bible

What Jesus said about the way · Bible Word God

Jesus is the way

Jesus said it plainly: "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me" (John 14:6). There is no list of human conditions that opens the door for us. The door is a person.

Born again

To Nicodemus he said: "Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God" (John 3:3). To be born again is to receive a new life that God gives through his Spirit when we believe in Christ.

A lived-out faith

Jesus also said: "Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven" (Matthew 7:21). True faith shows itself in obedience and love.

And here is the promise: "He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life" (John 5:24). Today you can begin: believe, pray and follow Jesus every day.

The narrow gate

Jesus said: "Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it" (Matthew 7:13-14). This gate is not a set of rules; it is Jesus himself, who said: "I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved" (John 10:9).

Narrow does not mean unreachable. The gate is open to all who want to enter. What Jesus asks is that we stop looking for other paths and trust in him alone.

Like children

The disciples asked who was the greatest in the kingdom, and Jesus placed a little child in their midst. He told them: "Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven" (Matthew 18:3). A child does not boast of merits; he trusts, asks and receives. This is how God wants us to come to him, without pride or excuses.

This is good news for those who feel unworthy. You do not need to have everything sorted out; it is enough to come humbly.

Repentance and faith

Jesus called all people to repent and believe. Zacchaeus, a corrupt tax collector, welcomed Jesus into his home and decided to repay what he had stolen. Jesus said: "This day is salvation come to this house" (Luke 19:9). Salvation cannot be bought, but it changes a life.

Likewise, when Jesus forgave the woman caught in sin, he said to her: "Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more" (John 8:11). Forgiveness and a new beginning go together.

First practical steps

If you want to enter through this door today, you can begin with a simple prayer, speaking to God from the depths of your heart. Then read the Gospel of John, one chapter each day. Look for a church that teaches the Bible and ask a brother who is mature in the faith to walk with you. And share your decision with someone close to you.

Jesus promised: "him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out" (John 6:37). This promise is for you today. It does not matter how long you have been away: he is waiting for you with open arms.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be baptized to be saved? Baptism is a command of Jesus and a public sign of faith (Matthew 28:19). Salvation is received by grace, through faith; baptism expresses that faith and obedience to Christ.

What if my family does not support me? Jesus said that following him could bring division, but he promised to be with us to the end (Matthew 28:20). Pray for them, live with love and seek the support of a church.

To reflect on this week

Think about what Jesus said about himself and honestly ask yourself whether you have put your trust in him.

Suggested verses: John 14:6, John 10:9, John 3:3-5, Matthew 7:13-14 and John 6:37. Read one each day, ask yourself what it teaches you about God and what concrete step you can take. Finish with a short prayer, giving thanks for what you have learned and asking for help to put it into practice. Sharing what you discover with a loved one or a friend helps you remember it and may encourage others.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you are the way. I want to follow you. Make me born again and teach me to obey you every day. Amen.
