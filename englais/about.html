About this site

Who we are

Biblia Palabra Dios is a Christian site created with a very simple idea: that anyone, anywhere, can open the Bible on their phone and read it without paying anything, without creating an account and without the reading being interrupted by ads. We are a small team that loves Scripture and wants to put it within everyone's reach.

We are not a denomination or a religious institution. We are believers who have seen how the Word of God comforts, corrects, encourages and transforms people's lives, and who wanted to build a simple place where that Word is always available, well organized and easy to find.

Our mission

Our mission is to bring the Bible closer to people with clarity and respect. We believe that Scripture is sufficient to guide the believer, and that is why our work consists of presenting it well: the complete text, well organized by books, chapters and verses, and accompanied by tools that help you read it more consistently.

We want those who come to the site for the first time to find a clear path, and those who already know the Bible to find a companion for their daily reading. Everything else, from the design to every new feature, is decided with this question: does this help the person read and understand the Word of God better?

What you will find here

You can read the Old Testament, with its thirty-nine books, and the New Testament, with its twenty-seven books. Each book opens into chapters and each chapter into verses, so you can go straight to the passage you are looking for. There is also a search box that lets you type a reference, for example John 3:16, and get there instantly.

In the Article of the Day section we publish a short text every day with verses, a prayer and a reflection to start or end your day. In addition, in the Articles section we are writing an explanation for each book of the Bible, beginning with the New Testament: who wrote it, who it was addressed to, what its central message is and which verses are worth memorizing.

For those who prefer to listen, the site offers audio reading of the chapters and lets you choose between different voices. It is a special help for people with low vision, for those who travel or work with their hands busy, and for those who simply want to let the Word sound in their home.

The Bible and our articles

The Bible text we offer is Scripture itself, and we do not add comments in the middle of the verses. Our articles and reflections are always presented separately, clearly set apart from the Bible text, so that no one confuses human words with the Word of God.

When we write about a book or a topic, we try to be faithful to the meaning of the passage, cite the references so you can check them, and avoid speculation. We invite you to do what the believers in Berea did: examine the Scriptures every day to see whether these things are so. If you find something we should correct, we will be glad to know.

Our values

Free. The Word of God is a gift, and that is how we want to offer it. Reading, searching and listening cost nothing.

Simple. We think of the phone first, because that is where most people visit us. Fewer buttons, clear text and fast navigation.

Respectful. We welcome people from different churches and backgrounds. Our focus is on Christ and Scripture, not on disputes between groups.

Transparent. We explain clearly how the site works, what data we use and how you can contact us. You can read more in our privacy policy.

How you can support this work

Keeping a site online, writing new articles, improving the audio and adding books has a cost in time and resources. That is why there is an offering button on the home page for those who wish to sustain this work. It is completely voluntary: reading the Bible will remain free for everyone, whether or not you make an offering.

You can also help in ways that cost no money: by sharing the site with a family member or a friend, by praying for this work, or by sending us your suggestions to improve it. Every person who reads the Word thanks to a recommendation from you is a fruit that matters.
