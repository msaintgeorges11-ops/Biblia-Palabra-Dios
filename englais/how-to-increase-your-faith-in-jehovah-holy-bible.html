📚 How to Increase Your Faith in Jehovah - Holy Bible

Practices the Bible recommends · Bible Word God

Listen to his Word

"So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God" (Romans 10:17). Reading the Bible every day, even just one chapter, nourishes faith. A faith without food grows weak.

Ask him for more faith

The disciples said to Jesus: "Increase our faith" (Luke 17:5). And that father cried out: "I believe; help thou mine unbelief" (Mark 9:24). Asking is a prayer that God hears.

Remember and persevere

Remember what God has done for you. The psalmist says: "I will remember the works of the LORD" (Psalm 77:11). James also teaches that the testing of faith produces patience (James 1:3).

Faith grows when it is put into practice: pray, obey and take small steps. Walk with other believers, who will encourage you when you grow weak.

Read the Bible regularly

Paul said that faith comes from hearing the Word of God. It is not about reading in a hurry to fulfill a duty, but about meditating. Choose a book, such as the Gospel of Mark or the Psalms, and read it slowly. Underline God's promises and write them down in a notebook.

The psalmist says that blessed is the man who meditates on the law of Jehovah day and night, and that he will be like a tree planted by streams of water (Psalm 1:2-3). Perseverance bears fruit with time.

Pray honestly

Faith grows when we talk with God. You do not need elegant words. Talk to him about your fears, your doubts and your joys. The Psalms teach that we can be very sincere with God, even voicing complaints, and end up trusting in him.

Write down your requests and the answers you see coming. Over the years, this notebook will become a testimony to God's faithfulness and will strengthen your faith in difficult times.

Walk with other believers

Faith is not cultivated in isolation. Hebrews 10:24-25 encourages us to watch over one another to stir up love and good works, without abandoning our gatherings. Hearing testimonies and praying together reminds us that God is at work in many lives.

If you are going through a hard time, ask someone to pray with you. Proverbs 27:17 says that iron sharpens iron, and a man sharpens the countenance of his friend.

Obey and wait

Faith also grows through obedience. When we take a step in line with the Word and see that God is faithful, our trust is strengthened. Start with something concrete: forgiving, giving, asking forgiveness or setting aside time for prayer.

And wait patiently. James 1:4 says that patience must have its perfect work, so that we may be perfect and complete, lacking nothing. Faith is like a muscle: it is strengthened by use. May you take a small step of trust today.

Frequently asked questions

How much time should I devote to it each day? The Bible gives no number, but it helps to set aside a fixed time, even ten or fifteen minutes. Regularity matters more than length.

What if I have doubts? Doubts are not a sin. Bring them to God, study Scripture and talk with more mature believers. Jude 22 says to have mercy on those who doubt.

To reflect on this week

Choose one habit for this week: read a chapter, pray for ten minutes or talk to someone about your faith. Do it consistently.

Suggested verses: Romans 10:17, Luke 17:5, James 1:2-4, Psalm 77:11-12 and Hebrews 10:24-25. Read one each day, ask yourself what it teaches you about God and what concrete step you can take. Finish with a short prayer, giving thanks for what you have learned and asking for help to put it into practice. Sharing what you discover with a loved one or a friend helps you remember it and may encourage others.

Prayer

Jehovah, my God, increase my faith. May your Word be my food and may my eyes be fixed on you. Amen.
