📚 How to Escape Hell According to the Holy Bible

What the Holy Bible says, step by step · Bible Word God

Recognize your need

The Bible says that "all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God" (Romans 3:23). Admitting it is not humiliating: it is the first step toward receiving help. No one is saved because they are better than someone else.

Believe in Jesus Christ

Jesus died and rose again to pay for our sins. "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house" (Acts 16:31). And also: "If thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved" (Romans 10:9).

Saved by grace

"For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast" (Ephesians 2:8-9). And the promise is firm: "There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus" (Romans 8:1).

Then walk with God: pray, read the Bible every day and look for a church where you can grow.

The problem of sin

To understand how to escape, we must understand what we are escaping from. Sin is not only making mistakes; it is living with our back turned to God. Isaiah says that "your iniquities have separated between you and your God" (Isaiah 59:2). By our own strength, we cannot repair that distance. No good work can erase the past.

That is why the Gospel is good news: what we could not do, God did. He sent his Son, who lived without sin, died on the cross for our sins and rose on the third day. This is the heart of the Christian message (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).

Truly repenting

Jesus began his ministry by saying: "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel" (Mark 1:15). To repent means to change your mind and your direction: to acknowledge your sin, to be sorry for it and to turn to God. It is not only feeling guilt, but deciding to walk with him.

John writes a precious promise: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness" (1 John 1:9). God never tires of forgiving the one who comes to him sincerely.

The assurance of salvation

Jesus said: "Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life" (John 5:24). Notice the verb: has passed. It is a present reality for the one who believes.

John also wrote his letter "that ye may know that ye have eternal life" (1 John 5:13). God does not want you to live in constant doubt, but with the peace of someone who rests on a promise that does not depend on his own strength.

The next steps

After believing, a new life begins. These simple steps will help you. First, pray every day, even with few words. Second, read the Bible, starting with the Gospel of John. Third, go to a church where the Word of God is taught and where you can grow with others. Fourth, tell someone you trust what happened in your heart.

Do not put this decision off. "Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation" (2 Corinthians 6:2). If you feel today that God is calling you, respond simply, just as you are. He will welcome you.

Frequently asked questions

What if I feel unworthy? No one is worthy by themselves. Jesus said that he did not come to call the righteous, but sinners (Mark 2:17). Your need is precisely the reason he calls you.

Can I lose my salvation if I fail again? We believers keep stumbling, but God is faithful to forgive. Paul says that nothing will be able to separate us from the love of God shown in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:38-39). If you fall, return to him quickly and confess.

To reflect on this week

If you have not yet taken the step, this week is a good time to talk to God simply and to look for someone to guide you.

Suggested verses: Romans 10:9-10, Ephesians 2:8-9, John 3:16, 1 John 1:9 and John 5:24. Read one each day, ask yourself what it teaches you about God and what concrete step you can take. Finish with a short prayer, giving thanks for what you have learned and asking for help to put it into practice. Sharing what you discover with a loved one or a friend helps you remember it and may encourage others.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, I acknowledge that I need you. Thank you for dying for me. Forgive my sins, come into my life and guide me. In your name, amen.
