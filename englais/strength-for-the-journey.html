Tonight

Theme: Strength for the Journey

Elijah Under the Broom Tree

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

1 Kings 19:4-8
Psalm 55:22

🙏 Today’s Prayer

My Lord and Father, as this day ends I come before you with a grateful heart. Thank you for the air I breathed, for the daily bread, and for the people I shared these hours with. Thank you that even in the hard moments, your hand did not let go of me. I acknowledge that everything good in this day came from you.

I confess, Lord, that today there was weariness too. There were moments when I felt without strength, wanting to give up, with the weight of things I could not resolve. Now I place in your hands the burdens I carried all day: the worries, the mistakes, the words I did not know how to say, and the tasks left undone. I do not want to take them with me to bed. You know how to care for me better than I know how to care for myself.

Father, give me true rest tonight. May my body be renewed and my mind be quieted. Watch over my family and all those I love, and keep also those who are alone, sick, or discouraged today. May I rise tomorrow with new strength, knowing that you nourish me and walk with me on the road. I lie down in peace, for you make me dwell in safety. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

The prophet Elijah had lived extraordinary days. He had seen the power of God on Mount Carmel, and the people had cried out that the Lord is the true God. But soon afterward, Queen Jezebel swore to take his life. Elijah, who had been so brave, was afraid and fled. He came to Beersheba, left his servant there, and went on alone, a day’s journey into the wilderness.

Exhausted and discouraged, he sat down under a broom tree and asked God to take his life. He said: It is enough; now, O Lord, take my life, for I am no better than my fathers. Then he lay down and fell asleep. He was a faithful man who had reached the limit of his strength. God did not rebuke him or leave him alone with his sorrow.

An angel touched him and said: Arise and eat. Elijah looked, and by his head was a cake baked on hot stones and a jar of water. He ate and drank and lay down again. The angel came a second time, touched him, and said: Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you. So it is told in chapter 19 of the first book of Kings.

Elijah got up, ate and drank, and in the strength of that food he walked forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mountain of God. Notice how God first cared for his weary body, with rest and food, before speaking to his heart. Sometimes what we need most at the end of a hard day is to sleep, eat something, and know that the Lord is still watching over us. He knows our limits and does not demand more of us than we can give in one day.

✝️ Today’s Message

Many times we think faith means always being strong and cheerful. Yet the Bible shows a great prophet like Elijah, sitting under a tree, worn out and with no desire to go on. This teaches us that deep weariness is not a sign of lack of faith. God’s servants also grow tired, get discouraged, and need to be lifted up. If you end the day this way today, do not feel guilty: you are in good company.

Notice how the Lord responded. He did not give Elijah a long speech or a list of reproaches. He sent him rest, food, and water, and a gentle touch that said: get up. God knows we are dust and attends to our real needs. When you are exhausted, taking care of your body, sleeping enough, and eating calmly is also a way of letting God sustain you.

King David wrote: Cast your burden on the Lord, and he will sustain you; he will never permit the righteous to be moved. To cast the burden means to truly let it go. Tonight, before you sleep, you can do a simple exercise: name each worry in prayer and say quietly: Lord, I hand this over to you. If you remember something else during the night, say it again. You do not have to resolve everything today.

And tomorrow, the same God who strengthened Elijah for a long journey will give you strength for yours. We will not always see the end of the road all at once, but we will have the nourishment needed for each stretch. Trust that the Lord will raise you up in his time. Sleep in peace: he who keeps you neither slumbers nor sleeps, and his faithfulness does not fail.

As you close this day, hand over to the Lord everything that weighed on your shoulders. He cares for you while you sleep and renews your strength for the road ahead. Rest calmly, because his love covers you all night long.
