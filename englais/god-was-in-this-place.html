Theme: God Was in This Place

Jacob’s Dream at Bethel

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Genesis 28:12-15
Isaiah 26:3-4

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord our God, as the day draws to a close, we come to you with gratitude. You have sustained us throughout the whole day, even in the moments when we did not notice. Like Jacob, who was fleeing and fell asleep with a stone for a pillow, we too discover that you were in this place and we did not know it. Thank you for your presence, which has not abandoned us for a single moment.

We thank you for today’s bread, for the kind words we received, for the doors that opened and also for those that closed, because we know you see farther than we do. Forgive us for the times we let ourselves be carried away by haste, complaint, or fear. What we could not settle today we leave in your hands, for you never grow weary and you do not sleep.

Tonight we hand over to you our burdens, our questions, and all those we love. Care for the sick, comfort those who weep, and give rest to those who cannot sleep. Keep our home by your peace, that peace which keeps the one who perseveres in you. May we wake tomorrow with hearts ready to say: surely the Lord was in this place. We rest on your faithfulness. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

Jacob was leaving home as a fugitive. He had deceived his brother Esau and was now traveling alone toward Haran, with no family, no security, and probably the weight of his own guilt. When the sun set, he came to a desolate place. He took one of the stones there, made it his pillow, and lay down to sleep.

Then he had a dream. He saw a ladder resting on the earth, with its top reaching to heaven, and the angels of God ascending and descending on it. And above it stood the Lord, who spoke to him. He did not reproach him for his past. He promised him the land where he lay, many descendants, and a blessing that would reach all the families of the earth. And he added the words Jacob needed most: “Behold, I am with you and will keep you wherever you go.”

Jacob awoke in fear and said: “Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it!” He took the stone he had used as a pillow, set it up as a monument, and poured oil on top of it. He called that place Bethel, which means house of God. What seemed a lonely place of flight became a place of encounter with the Lord.

Very often our nights resemble Jacob’s: weary, uncertain, far from what we would like. And it is precisely there that God draws near. He does not wait for us to be perfect before he speaks to us. He comes with promises, with his company, and with the certainty that he will not leave us until he has done what he has said. God did not leave Jacob in his loneliness: he visited him precisely when he needed it most and gave him a hope that no road could take from him, not even the fear of his own past.

✝️ Today’s Message

The story of Jacob at Bethel reminds us that God is not limited to temples or to happy moments. He appears on a dusty road, at night, before a man who did not deserve his favor. This encourages us to think that even if you did not feel it today, God was with you every hour of this day.

Before you sleep, take a simple look back over your day. Think of three moments when you saw the hand of God: an unexpected help, a strength you did not have, a kind word. Thank him for each one. Like Jacob, we sometimes discover God’s presence only when we stop to look back.

Then, hand over your burdens. The prophet Isaiah wrote that God keeps in perfect peace the one whose mind is stayed on him, because he trusts in him. Peace does not come from everything being settled, but from knowing in whom we rest. Whatever worried you today, a debt, a quarrel, an illness, say it aloud before the Lord and leave it in his hands.

Finally, set up your own stone of remembrance. Write one line in a notebook about what God did today. Tomorrow, when discouragement shows up, read it again. Trust in the Lord forever, Isaiah tells us, for the Lord is an everlasting rock. Sleep soundly: the one who keeps you does not slumber. And remember: where you least expect it, God can open a door to heaven for you.

Thank you, Lord, for this day that is drawing to a close. Leave in his hands what you could not finish, and rest in his peace, for he watches over your sleep. May his presence be with you all night long, and may you wake renewed. Rest in peace.
