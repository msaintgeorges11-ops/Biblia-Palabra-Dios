Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Biblia Palabra Dios we respect your privacy. This policy explains clearly what information may be involved when you use our site, what it is used for and what options you have. By browsing the site you accept what is described here. If you have any questions, you can write to us from the contact.

1. What information we collect

To read the Bible, search for verses, listen to the audio or read the articles, you do not need to register or give us any personal data. We do not ask for a name, email, phone number or password to use the content of the site.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us voluntarily, for example when you write to us by email or call us by phone. In that case we use your message, your name and your contact details only to reply to you.

2. Technical information

Like almost any website, the service that hosts our pages may record basic technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, the type of device and browser, the page visited and the date and time. This information is used so that the site works properly, to detect faults and to protect the service from abuse. We do not use it to identify you personally.

3. Data stored on your device

To offer you a better experience, the site may store small pieces of data in your device's own browser, for example the voice you chose for the audio or the last section you visited. This data stays on your phone or computer and is not used to track you. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings, although you will then have to choose your preferences again.

4. Audio and voices

The read-aloud feature for chapters uses the voices available on your device or browser. The voices you hear depend on your device and its system; we do not record or store your voice or what you listen to.

5. Offerings and third-party services

On the home page there is a button for those who wish to support this work with a voluntary offering. When you tap it, you will be taken to the PayPal site, which is an independent service with its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card or payment account details; that information is handled only by PayPal.

Our pages may contain links to other sites. We do not control their content or their privacy practices, so we recommend that you read their policies before providing them with information.

6. Cookies and advertising

A cookie is a small file that a site stores in your browser. Our site may use local storage technologies to remember your preferences. In addition, you can set your browser to block or delete cookies whenever you wish.

Google AdSense advertising. This site uses Google AdSense to display ads, which help keep the content free. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site and to other sites on the internet. The use of advertising cookies allows Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing.

You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's ad settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In some countries, the site may ask for your consent before using advertising cookies.

7. How we use the information

We use the information we receive only to: reply to your messages, keep the site running, improve its content and speed, and protect the service. When you write to us, your message is kept only so that we can assist you and follow up on your inquiry. We do not sell or rent your data. Third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, may collect information through cookies, as explained in section 6.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you entrust to us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection. We keep your messages only for as long as necessary to assist you and, if you request it, we delete them.

9. Minors

The content of the site is suitable for all audiences. We do not intentionally collect personal information from minors. If you are a mother, father or guardian and believe that a minor has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. To do so, get in touch with us and we will handle your request as soon as possible.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the applicable rules change. When we do, we will publish the new version here with its update date. We recommend that you review it from time to time.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or about your data, write to us at palabradios855@gmail.com or call us at 829-830-7028. We will be happy to help you.
