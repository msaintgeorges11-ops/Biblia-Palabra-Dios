Jehovah Is My Strength

Strength for every day in the Lord · Bible Word God

When Strength Runs Out

We all know weariness: physical, emotional, spiritual. There are seasons when going on costs a great deal. In those moments, one of the phrases most often repeated by the biblical authors becomes a remedy for the soul: “Jehovah is my strength.” It is not an empty slogan, but the testimony of those who experienced God’s help in very real circumstances.

In this article, we walk through some of those testimonies, so that you too can lean on the Lord.

The Song of Moses

After crossing the Red Sea and seeing Egypt defeated, Moses and the Israelites sang: “The LORD is my strength and song, and he is become my salvation” (Exodus 15:2). God’s strength was seen at the moment when his people were completely unable to save themselves.

This first mention teaches us that God’s strength is best displayed when we acknowledge our weakness. We do not need to have everything under control for God to act.

David’s Confession

David spent a great deal of time fleeing, hiding and facing enemies. And when he was delivered, he sang: “I will love thee, O LORD, my strength. The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer” (Psalm 18:1-2). God is not only a help, but a rock, a fortress, a shield.

In another psalm, he adds: “The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped” (Psalm 28:7). Trust produces help, and help produces praise.

The Joy of the Lord

In Nehemiah’s time, the people listened to the law and wept with sorrow over their sins. But Nehemiah told them: “The joy of the LORD is your strength” (Nehemiah 8:10). God does not want us to live in constant discouragement; he wants us, after repenting, to walk in the joy of his forgiveness.

The joy of the Lord does not depend on circumstances. It is a strength that is born from knowing that we are loved and forgiven.

Strength for the Discouraged

Isaiah offers a special promise to those who are weary: “they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint” (Isaiah 40:31). Trusting in Jehovah is not passivity, but active trust. It is keeping our eyes on God while we keep on walking.

And the same prophet declares: “Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid: for the LORD JEHOVAH is my strength and my song; he also is become my salvation” (Isaiah 12:2).

Even When Everything Fails

The prophet Habakkuk describes a situation with no harvest, no livestock and no human hope. And in spite of that, he declares: “Yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation. The LORD God is my strength” (Habakkuk 3:18-19). It is one of the most impressive declarations of faith in the Bible.

It is born not from the absence of problems, but from the presence of God in the midst of them.

How to Lean on His Strength

Paul learned that God’s power is made perfect in weakness (2 Corinthians 12:9). This means that you do not have to pretend to be strong. You can come before God just as you are and ask him to be your strength.

We encourage you to write “Jehovah is my strength” today and to place it where you will see it. Read it aloud when you feel tired. In time, you will find that the truth of this sentence takes root in your heart.

A Prayer for Strength

Lord Jehovah, you are my strength. Today I feel weak, but I trust that your power is made perfect in my weakness. Renew my strength, give me joy in the midst of trial and help me move forward one day at a time. Thank you because you never leave me alone. Hold me up when I am weary and remind me of your promises. In Jesus’ name, amen. You may read this prayer before you begin your day.

Remember also that God’s strength often reaches us through other people: a friend who listens, a church that prays, a word of encouragement at the right moment. Do not isolate yourself when you are weak. Let the Lord sustain you through his promises and through the company of his children, and soon you will be able to be the one who strengthens others with the same comfort you received.

Read These Passages in the Bible

  • Psalm 18:1-2 · My rock, my fortress
  • Nehemiah 8:10 · The joy of Jehovah
  • Isaiah 40:31 · Renewed strength
