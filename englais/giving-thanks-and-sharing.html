Theme: Giving Thanks and Sharing

The Feeding of the Five Thousand

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

John 6:8-11
John 6:12-13

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Good Father, at the end of this day I want to thank you for everything I have received from your hand. Thank you for food, for work, for conversations, for moments of laughter, and also for lessons that came disguised as difficulties. I recognize that nothing I have is only mine, for it is you who gave it to me.

Lord Jesus, as you took the loaves from that young boy and gave thanks before distributing them, teach me to place in your hands the little I have. Sometimes I feel that my time, my strength, and my resources are not enough. Today I give them to you again, convinced that in your hands what is small becomes sufficient. Forgive me if I let pass an opportunity to help someone, and give me the chance to do so tomorrow.

I pray for those who will go to bed tonight with hunger, loneliness, or fear. May bread and hope not be lacking for them, and use my hands, if you wish, to bring them some relief. I give you what has been left unresolved, for you gather even the pieces that remain, and nothing is lost with you. Grant me restful sleep and a calm heart. Thank you for your faithfulness that never fails. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

The Gospel of John tells how a great crowd followed Jesus beside the Sea of Galilee, because they saw the signs he performed on the sick. When it grew late, Jesus looked at the crowd and asked Philip where they could buy bread for them to eat. He did it to test him, for he already knew what he was going to do.

Philip answered with numbers: even two hundred denarii worth of bread would not be enough for each to have a little. Then Andrew, another disciple, pointed out that there was a boy there who had five barley loaves and two small fish. And he added an honest question: what are these for so many people? It was little, and the disciples knew it.

Jesus ordered the people to sit down on the grass. There were about five thousand men. Then he took the loaves, gave thanks, and distributed them, and did the same with the fish, as much as they wanted. Notice the order: first gratitude, then the miracle. Jesus did not wait to have more before giving thanks; he gave thanks for what there was.

When all were satisfied, Jesus asked that the leftover pieces be gathered, so that nothing would be lost. They filled twelve baskets with the pieces from the five barley loaves. The message is comforting: the little handed over to Jesus does not merely suffice; there is some left over. And God’s care extends even to what others would let be thrown away.

✝️ Today’s Message

The story of the boy with the five loaves holds a special lesson for evenings of gratitude. Often we end the day thinking about what was lacking: time, money, energy, patience. Andrew did the same when he asked what five loaves and two fish were for so many people. His question was sincere, but Jesus had another way of seeing scarcity.

Jesus took the little, gave thanks, and distributed it. See how much happens in that gesture. There was someone who agreed to give up his meal. There was a Master who gave thanks before seeing the result. And there were disciples who served the crowd with their own hands. This is how God works: he uses our small offering and fills it with his power, so that it is enough for all.

Tonight, instead of counting what you could not do, take an inventory of what you placed in God’s hands. A kind word, a simple service, a task finished honestly. All of it counts. And what you feel was not enough, you can hand to the Lord now, like those barley loaves. He knows how to multiply it in his time.

Notice also the twelve baskets of leftovers. God does not merely meet the need; he takes care of what remains. This speaks of a generous and orderly Father, who wastes nothing of what we give him. Before you sleep, give thanks for one concrete thing you received today, and think of someone with whom you could share something tomorrow. Gratitude grows when it is shared.

Rest now in the certainty that the Lord has cared for every detail of your day, even those you did not notice. Give thanks, let go of what weighs on you, and let his peace cover you. Sleep soundly, for the God who provides will keep providing tomorrow.
