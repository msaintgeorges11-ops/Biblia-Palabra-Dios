Theme: Here Am I, Send Me

The Burning Bush

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Exodus 3:10-12
Isaiah 6:6-8

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord and our God, we thank you for this new day and for the life you lend us. You are holy and at the same time near; you revealed yourself to Moses in a bush that burned without being consumed, and you touched Isaiah’s lips with a coal from the altar. Today we come to you with reverence, knowing that you know us by name and that you do not call us in order to leave us alone.

We acknowledge that, very often, we feel small and inadequate. Like Moses, we ask: who am I? But you answer: I will be with you. Purify our lips and our hearts of everything that gets in the way. Forgive our excuses and our fears. Give us attentive ears to hear your voice in the midst of the noise of the day.

Send us today, Lord, wherever you wish. May we know how to serve at home, at work, and in the street with humility and joy. Give us eyes to see the one who needs help and the courage to reach out a hand. Be yourself our strength when the task seems too great, and remind us that what matters is not so much what we have, but that you go with us. Here we are; use us for your glory. Here we are, Lord, ready to listen to you today. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

Moses had been a shepherd for forty years in the land of Midian. He had grown up in the palace of Egypt, but now he was tending the sheep of his father-in-law Jethro, far from his people. One day he led the flock to the mountain of God, called Horeb, and there he saw a strange sight: a bush that was burning with fire but was not consumed.

He went over to look, and God called him by name from the midst of the bush: “Moses, Moses!” He answered, “Here I am.” God told him to take off his sandals, for the place where he stood was holy ground. Then he explained that he had seen the affliction of his people in Egypt, that he had heard their cry, and that he had come down to deliver them. And then he said to him: “Now go, I am sending you to Pharaoh, so that you may bring my people out of Egypt.” God did not need Moses to be strong, but to be obedient, and that is why he chose him in spite of his doubts and his years.

Moses made objections. He asked who he was to go before Pharaoh. God did not argue about his smallness; he answered with a promise: “I will be with you.” Moses’ assurance did not rest on his ability, but on God’s company.

Centuries later, the prophet Isaiah lived through a similar scene in the temple. He felt lost before the holiness of the Lord, but a seraph touched his lips with a burning coal and assured him that his sin was forgiven. Then he heard the voice of God: “Whom shall I send?” And Isaiah answered: “Here am I; send me.” In both accounts, God purifies, calls, and accompanies the one who makes himself available. It is a story the people of Israel told for generations: the God who sees, who hears, and who comes down to deliver his own never calls anyone only to abandon them along the way.

✝️ Today’s Message

God still calls today. He does not always do it through a burning bush; sometimes it is through a need that comes your way, through an opportunity to serve, or through a whisper in the heart. The question is whether we are attentive and willing.

First, do not make excuses for your weakness. Moses said he was not eloquent, and God gave him his brother Aaron as a helper. What God asks for is availability, not perfection. If today you feel that you are not up to what has been entrusted to you, remember the promise: “I will be with you.”

Second, let God purify you. Isaiah was first forgiven, then sent. Before you leave to work or to serve, confess to God what weighs on your conscience and receive his forgiveness. A purified heart serves with joy, not with pride or as a burden.

Third, say “here I am” in a practical way. Think of just one person you can serve today: an elderly neighbor, a coworker who is struggling, a relative who is alone. Do it without expecting applause. God uses simple gestures to accomplish great things. As you begin this day, repeat in your heart the words of Isaiah: “Here am I; send me.” And walk with confidence, for the one who calls you goes with you. May this day find you attentive to the voice of God and ready to obey, with the humility of one who knows that power does not come from us.

May the God who called Moses and Isaiah give you courage and joy to serve today. Walk with confidence, for his presence goes with you at every step. May his peace and strength accompany you from the morning on. God is taking care of you today.
