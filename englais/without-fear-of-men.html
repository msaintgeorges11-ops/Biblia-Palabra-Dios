Theme: Without Fear of Men

The Death of John Knox

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Isaiah 51:12-13
Exodus 14:13-14

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Almighty God, at the beginning of this day, we thank you, for you are our comforter and our refuge. You say in your Word: I, I am he who comforts you; who are you, that you fear mortal man? Today we want to believe these words with all our hearts and walk free from the fear that paralyzes.

Lord, you know the fears we carry this morning: what people will say, criticism, the uncertainty of work, health, the future of our children. We place them in your hands. As to the people by the sea, you tell us: fear not, stand firm, and see the salvation of the Lord, which he will work for you today. You fight for us, and we can be still in you.

Give us the courage to speak the truth in love and to do what is right even if it is not popular. Free us from pride and harshness, and make us at once humble, gentle, and firm. May our fear be for you alone, the beginning of wisdom. Guide our steps, protect our family, and bless the work of our hands. Walk with us this day. May your power always sustain us and your love guide us, Lord, today and always. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

On November 24, 1572, the reformer John Knox died in Edinburgh, Scotland. He was one of the leaders of the Protestant Reformation in his country. For years he preached with great boldness and, because of his faith, did not hesitate to speak frankly to kings and queens. His ministry left a deep mark on the Church of Scotland.

His last days were days of weakness. On November 9, 1572, he installed James Lawson as his successor in St Giles’ church in Edinburgh, and went home for the last time. There, surrounded by friends and a few of the kingdom’s most important nobles, he asked that the Bible be read aloud. On the day of his death, his young wife read to him from Paul’s first letter to the Corinthians.

He was buried in the churchyard of St Giles’, and the nobles of Scotland attended his funeral. Beside the grave, James Douglas, Earl of Morton, recently appointed regent of the kingdom, spoke the words that remained in the memory of the people: “Here lies one who never feared any man.”

That sentence did not describe a man without fears, but someone who had learned that the fear of God frees us from the fear of men. Knox was not perfect, and his character was sometimes harsh. But his life teaches that the believer who rests in God can speak and act with firmness, without being overpowered by the pressure of the powerful or the opinion of the crowd. Today his memory invites us to ask God for the same firmness, to speak the truth with conviction and with respect.

✝️ Today’s Message

The sentence spoken beside John Knox’s grave makes us think of how many times our decisions are guided by fear of what people will say. Isaiah asked the people: who are you, that you fear mortal man? And he reminded them that they had forgotten the Lord, their Maker.

First, identify your fear for today. Is it a difficult conversation, a meeting, a decision you have put off? Name it before God. A fear that has a name loses some of its strength. Remember that the one who comforts you is the same one who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundations of the earth.

Second, put into practice what Moses said by the sea: “Fear not, stand firm, and see the salvation of the Lord, which he will work for you today.” Standing firm does not mean doing nothing; it means trusting and obeying. Diligently do what falls to you, and leave in God’s hands what you cannot control.

Third, live with integrity. Knox read the Bible until the last day of his life. Today, before you go out, read a few verses and ask God for a word for your day. A clear conscience and a mind filled with Scripture give an assurance that no applause can give. And treat others with respect: Christian courage must always be joined to love. Walk today fearing no one, but revering God. If anyone presses you today to do something that does not please God, pray before you answer and ask for wisdom, for God gives to all generously and without reproach.

May the Lord, your comforter, keep your heart from all fear and give you steadfastness for this day. Walk with your head held high, knowing that he fights for you. May his peace and blessing go with you at every step. Trust in him today.
