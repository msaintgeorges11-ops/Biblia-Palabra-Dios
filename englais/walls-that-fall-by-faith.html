Theme: Walls That Fall by Faith

The Fall of the Walls of Jericho

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Joshua 6:2-5
Hebrews 11:30

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Eternal God, thank you for letting me see the beginning of a new day. I acknowledge that you are the one who sustains my life and that every morning is a sign of your mercy. Place me in your hands today and teach me to walk according to your will and not according to my fears.

Lord, sometimes I find myself before walls that seem impossible: problems that will not resolve, wounded relationships, habits I cannot manage to change. Like Israel before Jericho, I do not have the strength to bring them down by myself. But you told Joshua that you had given the city into his hands. Give me the faith to believe that what you promise, you fulfill, and the patience to obey even when your ways seem strange to me. Thank you, Lord, because you never tire of sustaining me, and because your faithfulness is greater than any obstacle I will meet today.

Help me to be steady in what you ask of me today, even if it seems small. May I not be discouraged if I do not see results right away. May my trust rest not on what I see, but on who you are. Keep my steps, bless my work, and give me a heart willing to serve others with joy. May I remember today that victory comes from you and not from my own strength. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

After crossing the Jordan, the people of Israel found themselves before Jericho, a closed and fortified city. The Bible says that its gates were tightly shut because of the Israelites, and that no one went out or came in. Humanly speaking, there was no way to take it.

Then God gave Joshua a plan unlike any battle plan. For six days the men of war were to march around the city once a day, and seven priests would carry trumpets of ram’s horns before the ark. On the seventh day, they would march around the city seven times, and the priests would blow the trumpets. When they heard the long blast, all the people were to shout a great shout, and the wall of the city would fall down.

Imagine what it must have been like to walk in silence around those walls, day after day, without seeing any change. But the people obeyed. On the seventh day, after the seventh circuit, Joshua told the people to shout, for the Lord had given them the city. The people shouted, the trumpets sounded, and the wall fell down, so that everyone went straight up into the city and they captured it.

Before the conquest, near the city, Joshua had been visited by a man with a drawn sword in his hand, who introduced himself as the commander of the army of the Lord. Joshua fell facedown to the ground and worshiped, and he was told to take off his sandals, for the place was holy. The battle belonged to the Lord.

The book of Hebrews sums up this story in one short sentence: by faith the walls of Jericho fell, after they had been encircled for seven days. They did not fall by the strength of the army, but by the faith that obeys what God had said.

✝️ Today’s Message

Jericho teaches us that God does not always act the way we expect. An army would have attacked with ladders and battering rams, but God asked them to walk, to wait, and to trust. Sometimes the hardest part of faith is not action, but obedient waiting when you see no results.

Think about the people during those six days. Every lap around the city seemed to change nothing. The walls stayed just as high. And yet every step counted. It is the same with many good things in our lives: praying for a family member, persevering in honest work, forgiving little by little, being consistent in caring for our children. It seems as if nothing is happening, but God is at work.

Today you may ask yourself what your wall is. Perhaps a debt, an illness, a family situation, or a fear that has been with you for a long time. You do not have to bring it down with your own strength. Your part is to obey what God shows you: pray, speak sincerely, do what is right, take the next step, and wait in faith.

And remember that the people also shouted. When God’s time came, they expressed their faith with joy. Today, instead of complaining about what is lacking, give thanks for what God has already done and declare with your mouth that he is faithful. Obedient and grateful faith is the faith that sees walls fall in their time. This same spirit can go with you all week long: patient obedience, steady trust, and sincere gratitude.

May you walk today with the confidence that God goes before you. Every step of obedience, even the smallest, is in his hands. Go out with courage, for the Lord is faithful and cares for you in everything you do.
