Theme: Today Salvation Has Come

Zacchaeus in the Sycamore

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Luke 19:5-6
Luke 19:9-10

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Lord Jesus, at the end of this day, we thank you because you seek and save the lost. Thank you, because you pass along our road, you look up, and you call us by name. You do not look at us the way the world looks at us, but with a gaze of love that transforms the heart. Praise be to you for your patience with us.

Today, Lord, we want to receive you with joy in our home, as Zacchaeus did. Come into our household, into our thoughts, and into our plans. Show us what we must correct, what we must return, and what we must forgive. Give us an honest heart that does not hide behind the crowd or appearances. Thank you for every opportunity you gave us today to act with justice.

Tonight we place in your hands the worries we could not settle and the people we carry in our hearts. Take care of those who feel alone or rejected, of those whom no one seeks or invites. May they, too, hear your voice saying: today I want to stay at your house. Grant us rest, Lord, and peaceful sleep, for you have had mercy on us. In Jesus’ name, amen. May your grace always reach us, Lord, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

Jesus entered Jericho and was passing through the city. There lived a man named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector and very rich. Tax collectors gathered taxes for the Roman empire, and people despised them, because many demanded more than was due. Zacchaeus was a powerful man, but surely a very lonely one.

He was trying to see who Jesus was, but he could not because of the crowd, for he was short in stature. So he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to see him when he passed by. He had no idea what was about to happen. When Jesus came to that spot, he looked up and said to him: “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down; for I must stay at your house today.” Climbing a tree was undignified for an important man, but Zacchaeus did not stop to think about what people would say, because his desire to see the Lord was stronger than his shame.

Zacchaeus came down at once and received him joyfully. The people muttered, because Jesus had gone in to be the guest of a sinner. But Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord: “Look, Lord, half of my possessions I give to the poor, and if I have cheated anyone of anything, I will pay back four times as much.”

Jesus answered: “Today salvation has come to this house, because this man, too, is a son of Abraham. For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.” The encounter with Jesus changed Zacchaeus from the inside. It was neither the law nor pressure from people that transformed him, but the grace of having been sought and accepted. That day, Zacchaeus went from being a rejected man to a son of Abraham recognized by Jesus in front of everyone.

✝️ Today’s Message

The story of Zacchaeus teaches us that Jesus takes the initiative. While Zacchaeus was climbing the tree out of curiosity, Jesus had already seen him and had his name ready. Tonight you can rest knowing that it is not you who are chasing God in desperation: he is the one who seeks you first.

First, think about what you did today to draw near to Jesus. Perhaps a quick prayer, a reading from the Bible, or an act of kindness. Even if it was small, God saw it. Zacchaeus only climbed a tree, and the Lord stopped.

Second, examine whether there is anything you need to make right. Zacchaeus was not content with the emotion of the encounter; he offered to give to the poor and to repair the wrong he had done. This was not how he bought his salvation, for Jesus had already said that salvation had come to his house. His conduct was the fruit of a changed heart. If you have wronged someone by words or actions, decide to ask forgiveness tomorrow and to repair what you can.

Third, look at others as Jesus looked at them. All around us there are people whom others have pushed aside, like Zacchaeus. Ask God to give you eyes tomorrow to see one of them and the courage to approach. And now, rest. The Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost, and that includes you. Sleep in joy, for today, too, salvation has come to your house. May your heart be at peace.

Thank you, Lord, for this day in which your grace reached us. Place in his hands whatever was left unfinished and rest in the joy of knowing you are sought and loved. May your home sleep today under his peace. May your night be blessed with true rest.
