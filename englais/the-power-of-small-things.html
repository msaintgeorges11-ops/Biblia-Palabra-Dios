🌅 This morning

Theme: The Power of Small Things

Billy Graham

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

Matthew 13:31-32
Psalm 71:17-18

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Heavenly Father, I thank you for this new day you give me. I open my eyes and recognize that every morning is a sign of your faithfulness. Your mercies have not come to an end, and your love has held me since before I woke. Today I come before you with a simple heart, wanting to listen to you before I go out to do my tasks.

Lord, sometimes I feel small and think that what I do does not matter. Teach me to see with your eyes. You take what is small and make it grow; you use the humble to carry your light to others. Give me courage today to sow a good word, a kind gesture, a quiet prayer for someone who needs it, even if no one sees. Keep my tongue from complaining and fill my hands with service.

Give me wisdom for the decisions of this day, patience with the people I meet, and faith to trust that you are at work even when I see no results. Watch over my family, my friends, and those who are suffering at this moment. May my whole day be pleasing before you, and when it ends may I be able to say that I walked at your side. I place in your hands my plans, my fears, and my hopes. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

On November 7, 1918, a boy named William Franklin Graham was born in Charlotte, North Carolina, whom the world would come to know as Billy Graham. No one could have imagined then that this boy, raised in the simple setting of a dairy farm, would go on to preach the gospel to multitudes in many countries.

As a young man, Billy embraced faith in Jesus Christ and felt the call to proclaim his name. He studied at the Florida Bible Institute, was ordained in 1940 by the Southern Baptist Convention, and graduated from Wheaton College in Illinois in 1943. Over the years, his crusades carried the message of the cross to cities around the world, and his invitation was always the same: to return to God and trust in Christ as Savior.

Billy Graham died in February 2018, at the age of 99. Millions of people remember him not so much for his fame as for his insistence that the Bible is the center of everything and that Jesus is the only way to the Father. His life began in a humble place and ended by leaving a mark on entire generations.

His story reminds us of what Jesus taught with a tiny seed. God does not need to begin with something big to do something big. Many times he takes an ordinary life, given steadily, and turns it into a blessing for multitudes. What matters is not the size of the starting point, but the faithfulness with which we respond when God calls us.

✝️ Today’s Message

The story of Billy Graham reminds us that God does not need to begin with something big. A farm boy, a young man who said yes to Christ’s call, and a life given steadily. Jesus taught this same truth when he compared the kingdom of heaven to a mustard seed, the smallest of all seeds, which grows into a tree where the birds of the air make their nests.

Perhaps today you feel that what you have is little: a simple job, a humble home, a voice that almost no one hears. But God does not measure as we measure. The psalmist expressed it with gratitude: “O God, you have taught me from my youth, and to this day I declare your wondrous deeds.” Everyone who has been taught by God has something to tell about his wonders, and that testimony is a seed.

How can you apply it today? First, sow something small: write an encouraging message to someone, share a word of hope, help a person without expecting anything in return. Second, pray for someone around you who does not yet know the love of Jesus, and ask for an opportunity to speak to them with respect and simplicity. Third, do not compare yourself with anyone; faithfully do what God has placed in your hands.

And remember that growth belongs to God. We plant, and he makes it grow. You can head into your day without the pressure of seeing immediate results. It is enough to be faithful, to be kind, and to trust. A seed planted in love today may, in time, become a tree that gives shade to many.

May the Lord bless your morning and go with you at every step of the way. Go out with the certainty that you do not walk alone and that even the smallest thing you do in love has value to God. Trust in him, and may his peace guard your heart.
