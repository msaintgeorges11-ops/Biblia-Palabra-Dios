🌅 This morning

Theme: A Treasure in Jars of Clay

Angelo Roncalli, John XXIII

⏱ Approximate duration: 5 min

📖 Today’s Verses

2 Corinthians 4:7
Romans 12:16

🙏 Today’s Prayer

Heavenly Father, I thank you for the gift of this morning. You woke me with life, with breath, and with the chance to begin again. Before the noise of the day reaches me, I want to pause a moment before you and acknowledge that everything I am and everything I have comes from your generous hand.

Lord, you know how fragile I am. You know my limits, my weariness, and my hidden fears. Today I do not want to pretend to a strength I do not have. I want to remember that you have entrusted a treasure to me in a jar of clay, so that it may be seen that the power comes from you and not from me. Teach me to live simply, without seeking the places of honor, and to treat with affection those who seem small in the eyes of the world.

Guide my words, my steps, and my decisions. Help me to listen before speaking, to serve before demanding, and to smile even if no one thanks me for it. Place in my path people who need a kind word, and give me a heart willing to offer it. When this day is over, may I be able to say that I walked with you, with humility and with peace. Watch over my family, my friends, and all who carry burdens today that I cannot see. In Jesus’ name, amen.

📚 Today’s Story

On November 25, 1881, Angelo Roncalli was born in Sotto il Monte, a small village near Bergamo in northern Italy. His family were sharecroppers, peasants who worked land that was not their own and lived with only the bare necessities. There were thirteen brothers and sisters, and the household held together through the work of all, shared prayers, and a simple faith passed down from parents to children.

Nothing in that home suggested the boy would go far. But Angelo felt a desire to serve God very early on. He studied for the priesthood, was ordained in 1904, and for many years carried out quiet assignments, far from the headlines. He served in various countries, and those who knew him remembered his warm manner, his good humor, and the way he listened to each person as if they were the most important one.

Already advanced in age, he was elected bishop of Rome in 1958 and took the name John XXIII. Many thought he would be a transitional pope, an elderly man who would barely keep things as they were. Yet a few months later he announced that he was calling a great council, hoping that the Church would speak to the world with clarity and closeness. He died on June 3, 1963, and many still remember him as a kindly shepherd.

Roncalli’s story needs no embellishment. It reminds us that God often works with people of humble origin, who do not seem important and do not think themselves important. It is the same truth the apostle Paul wrote: we carry the treasure of the Gospel in jars of clay. What is precious is not the jar, but what God has placed in it.

✝️ Today’s Message

Today I want to talk to you about something we sometimes forget: your worth does not depend on the apparent greatness of your life. Paul wrote to the Corinthians that we have this treasure in jars of clay. A clay jar is ordinary, it can break, and it impresses no one. Yet God decided to put in it what is most precious: his light and his love.

This changes the way we begin the day. If you feel small, tired, or poorly prepared, you are not disqualified. On the contrary, you are right where God’s strength shows most clearly. He does not look for perfect jars; he looks for available ones. The life of Angelo Roncalli, born into a peasant family, shows us that simplicity is no obstacle to God using us.

Paul also advised the Romans not to be proud in their thinking, but to be drawn to what is lowly, and not to be wise in their own eyes. It is very practical advice. Someone you live with needs you today to treat them as an equal. It might be a coworker whom almost no one greets, an elderly neighbor, a child who asks too many questions, or a relative who feels forgotten.

Here are three steps for today. First, as you begin, say to God sincerely: Lord, I am made of earth, but I am yours. Second, look around you for a simple person and give them real time, without watching the clock or your phone. Third, when something succeeds, give the credit to God and to those who helped you. That way the treasure will keep shining, and the clay will stay in its place, serving with joy.

May the Lord fill your day with serenity and good cheer. Walk simply today, knowing that God sustains what seems small and fragile. His grace is sufficient for you, and his company will not fail you at any moment of this day.
